    Practical checks02 / Use cases

    What you can check in the PDF

    1. 01

      Check the approximate label size

      Draw a rectangle around the keyline to read its approximate width and height. If the keyline has its own channel, isolate it to make the outline easier to see.

    2. 02

      Identify the inks used

      Review the CMYK coverage and named spot channels, then isolate channels to see where each ink is used in the artwork.

    3. 03

      Locate foil or varnish areas

      If foil or varnish is supplied as a named spot channel, isolate it to see the intended areas. Use the black preview for light swatches that are hard to see in the composite.

    4. 04

      Estimate a spot-color label

      Read each ink’s coverage over the label area and carry the figures into your estimating workflow.

    5. 05

      Compare artwork versions

      Measure the same area in each version to understand how the ink coverage changes.

    6. 06

      Investigate a dense patch

      Use the ink-limit overlay to find it, then zoom in and inspect the individual channels. Exclude white, foil or varnish channels when they do not belong in the limit being checked.

    Usage notes03 / Quick tips

    Working with selections and channels

    Measure the label, rather than the surrounding page

    Blank space is part of the average. Selecting the label’s keyline gives you coverage figures for that rectangle and a rough size estimate.

    Alt-click an ink to see it on its own

    In the analysis pane, hold Alt and click a channel to isolate it. Repeat the Alt-click to restore all channels. Use “Channel preview in black” for very light inks.

    Choose which inks count toward your limit

    Leave white, foil or varnish out when your chosen ink limit does not include them. The check and report record which inks you included.

    How to Use InkCoverage

    1

    Upload a PDF

    Choose PDF or drag a PDF onto the upload area (up to 50 MB and 100 pages). Processing takes place on the server. New previews and analyses are available for 10 minutes from upload; completed results remain available in this browser until you close or replace the document or change the selection. See File handling and privacy for details.

    2

    Choose a page and viewing scale

    Use the page controls to move through the document. Fit, 150%, 200% and 500% change the viewing scale, not the measurement. Use Hand mode to move a zoomed page; on a keyboard you can also hold Space while dragging.

    3

    Select an area if needed

    Drag on the preview and adjust the corner handles. The dimensions give an approximate size of the selected area, useful for checking a label or keyline. Without a selection, the whole page is measured. Reset the selection to return to the full page.

    4

    Analyze average coverage

    Analyze Ink Coverage opens a dialog for the current page, all pages or a page range. A selection applies at the same position and size on each chosen page; pages that cannot contain it are skipped. Review the completion summary if any pages fail or are skipped.

    5

    Read the averages

    Each ink value is its average across the measured area, including blank space and tints. CMYK, spot and all-channel sums are listed separately and can exceed 100%. These averages are not the highest local ink total. Use Check ink limit to look for concentrated heavy coverage.

    6

    Check an ink limit

    Check ink limit works on the current page or its selected area. Set your target percentage and choose which inks count. Exclude white, foil, varnish or technical channels when appropriate. The results show the percentage of the checked area above the limit and the highest total found among the included inks.

    7

    Inspect the map and individual channels

    Heatmap shows only the ink-limit map on a blank page; Overlay shows it over the artwork. Click the active mode to return to the document. In the ink list, use checkboxes to hide or show channels. Alt-click one channel to isolate it; Alt-click it again to restore all. Channel preview in black makes light inks easier to see. Viewing choices do not change measurements or ink-limit exclusions.

    8

    Copy or download completed results

    Copy, CSV and Report include all completed page measurements, even while you are viewing a different page. Report downloads a PDF with page previews, measured dimensions, ink averages and completed ink-limit checks. Incomplete batches are identified. Profile information stays in exports and reports. Channel visibility does not remove inks from these results.

    Coverage can support ink-consumption estimates, but does not directly calculate ink mass or cost and does not certify print readiness. These also depend on the ink, substrate and printing process. Confirm production limits with your printer and use suitable preflight checks.
