Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. PDF processing and expiry

Your PDF is uploaded to our server and processed with Ghostscript. The file, page geometry and requested selection are used to create previews and calculate coverage. PDFs and temporary images are not sent to an analytics service. No account is needed.

2. Access expiry and cleanup

New previews and analyses expire 10 minutes from the start of upload. Activity does not extend this window. Cleanup runs on startup and every 60 seconds. Temporary outputs are normally removed when processing finishes; leftovers are retried by cleanup. Expired originals are normally removed on the first cleanup after expiry. Active work can finish first. Deletion failures are logged and retried, so physical deletion may take longer; there is no guaranteed physical deletion deadline. Closing only clears the browser view. Completed matching results remain exportable on their pages until you close or replace the document or change the selection. Upload the PDF again for new work.

3. Cookies, browser preferences and outcomes

Browser localStorage saves language, measurement units and your Analytics choice. It does not save PDFs or analysis results. Google Analytics is optional and loads only after you allow it. Google receives page visits, tool actions and ordinary connection/browser information, using analytics cookies to distinguish visits. We do not send artwork, filenames, customer details, entered text or calculated values. Advertising features are disabled. Your choice and analytics cookies last up to 180 days. Use Analytics choices at the bottom of any page to change your choice; declining keeps the tools available. Actions before permission and built-in examples are not included in tool-use tracking. Documents, selections, coverage results and contact text are not saved there by the application. Preview images and completed results are held in the current page’s memory; copied text and downloaded CSV files remain on your device until you remove them. Aggregate counts of completed, rejected, failed and interrupted upload, preview and analysis requests are held in server memory and periodically written to operational logs. These counts contain no filename, document ID, page number/count, geometry, ink name, coverage value, IP address or browser identifier.

4. Rate limiting and operational logs

The application uses IP addresses in memory to enforce shared request allowances. Records are reclaimed after the rolling one-hour window, on subsequent requests, or when the server restarts. Application access logging is disabled in the supplied launchers. Cleanup and aggregate outcome logs contain operational counts and status information. Hosting, reverse-proxy and Cloudflare security/access logs may include IP addresses, request metadata, timestamps and response status. Their retention is managed separately from the PDF expiry window; we do not claim a verified deletion period for those logs. Do not put confidential details in URLs.

5. Network transport and hosting

The site is delivered through Cloudflare on hosting provided by Hetzner. The main site inkcoverage.app and the separate beta.inkcoverage.app use HTTPS from your browser to Cloudflare and certificate-validated HTTPS from Cloudflare to the origin. The main-site configuration was verified on 16 October 2026. A local development preview uses loopback HTTP. Cloudflare handles traffic passing through its service and may apply security cookies according to its settings.

6. Optional contact

The contact form sends the message and optional reply email directly to Web3Forms when you press Send message. Web3Forms forwards submissions for us to read and reply to. Network metadata may also be processed by that service. Contact messages and email correspondence are separate from PDF processing and are not subject to its 10-minute expiry. The configured Web3Forms account retention and recipient-mailbox deletion schedule have not been independently verified. No PDF or measured result is attached automatically. Do not send confidential artwork or sensitive personal information in your message.

7. External links and providers

Cloudflare and Hetzner provide delivery/hosting. Web3Forms is used only for optional contact submission. Ko-fi is an optional external support link; GitHub and LabelEstimate are external links. They are not embedded tracking widgets and are contacted when you choose to visit them. Their own privacy terms apply. The application does not display advertisements. Provider policies: Cloudflare, Hetzner, Web3Forms, Ko-fi.

8. Questions and changes

Use the contact form for a privacy question or a request about correspondence you sent. A reply email is optional but needed if you want a response. You can remove browser preferences through your browser’s site-data settings. This page is updated when handling changes. The source is available on GitHub.
